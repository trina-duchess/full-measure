How to log

Drag a food from the list into a meal box to add one serving. Drop it again to add another, or use − and +. Tap × to remove it. On a phone or tablet, press and hold a food for a moment, then drag.

The tracker puts the nutrient you're furthest from meeting at the top. Limits (sodium, added sugar, caffeine) are listed separately, since less is better there.

Where the targets come from

Daily targets are the National Academies' Dietary Reference Intakes for women 51–70 (RDA, or AI where no RDA exists), as published in the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets. Sodium and added-sugar limits come from the Dietary Guidelines for Americans; the caffeine limit is FDA guidance. Food values come from USDA FoodData Central and your own product labels. Iodine values are approximate, and the pasta is an estimate.
